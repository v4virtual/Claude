# Claude sessions

Work produced in Claude Code sessions. Each session gets its own folder. Everything is also published with GitHub Pages at <https://v4virtual.github.io/Claude/>.

| Folder | What it is | Live (GitHub Pages) |
|--------|------------|------|
| [`caesar-cipher-animation/`](caesar-cipher-animation/) | Animated, interactive explainer of the Caesar cipher: ten chapters from history to frequency analysis, plus a cipher workbench. Single HTML file. | [Open](https://v4virtual.github.io/Claude/caesar-cipher-animation/) |
| [`conservation-of-mass/`](conservation-of-mass/) | Interactive IB MYP Year 10 explainer on chemical reactions and the law of conservation of mass: equation balancer, open vs closed system experiments and a self-check quiz. Single HTML file. | [Open](https://v4virtual.github.io/Claude/conservation-of-mass/) |
| [`mahoot/`](mahoot/) | Mahoot: learn, practise and battle your class, by year, subject and topic. Year 10 Math: exponent laws (visual lessons, 24 random question types). Year 8 Math: decimals (moved here from `decimal-quiz/`, five difficulty levels). Random quizzes with a worked solution after every answer, and a Kahoot-style live class battle (room code, link or QR, timed questions, leaderboard) on Firebase. | [Open](https://v4virtual.github.io/Claude/mahoot/) |
| [`mitosis/`](mitosis/) | Kurzgesagt-style animated video (7:58, 1080p) on mitosis for a Year 8 / MYP 3 student: PMAT, the cell cycle, interphase, chromatin, spindle fibers, cytokinesis and more, with narration, music, subtitles, a script, glossary, and a random quiz site built from the video's animation. | [Watch video](https://v4virtual.github.io/Claude/mitosis/mitosis-explainer.mp4) · [Interactive player](https://v4virtual.github.io/Claude/mitosis/animation.html) · [Random quiz](https://v4virtual.github.io/Claude/mitosis/quiz/) |
