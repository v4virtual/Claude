# Nothing Lost, Nothing Created

An interactive explainer for IB MYP Year 10 (MYP 5) Sciences on chemical reactions and the law of conservation of mass. Single HTML file, no build step, no dependencies apart from Google Fonts.

Open `index.html` in a browser.

| Part | Section | What it covers |
|------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | What counts as a chemical reaction? | Five signs of a reaction; physical vs chemical change |
| 2 | The law of conservation of mass | Lavoisier's sealed-vessel experiments and why atoms conserve mass |
| 3 | Balance it (interactive) | Adjust coefficients for five reactions; particle picture, atom tally and a mass balance that only levels when the equation is balanced |
| 4 | Open vs closed systems (interactive) | Run four experiments on a digital balance: burning magnesium, marble + acid open and sealed, a precipitation reaction |
| 5 | Calculations | Worked example: 0.24 g Mg + O₂ → 0.40 g MgO |
| 6 | Misconceptions | Five common myths and the correct explanations |
| 7 | Check yourself | Four multiple-choice questions and a calculation with feedback |

Ends with a key-vocabulary glossary and MYP framework links (key concept, related concepts, global context, statement of inquiry). Masses use simplified relative atomic masses (H = 1, C = 12, O = 16, Na = 23, Mg = 24, Cl = 35.5, Ca = 40, Fe = 56).

It honours `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-color-scheme`, and works down to phone width.
